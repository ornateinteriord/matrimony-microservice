# Girija Kalyana Matrimony (apps/Girija-Kalyana)

> Girija Kalyana is a premium sacred matrimonial platform dedicated to community-based matrimonial matchmaking in Karnataka and across India, featuring verified profiles, community filters, and high-trust privacy controls.

## Architecture
- Framework: React 18 with Vite 6 and Material UI (MUI v6)
- Theme: Sacred Rosewood (#881337) and Antique Gold (#D97706)
- Data Compliance: Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 compliant data governance
- Backend Service: Express.js + Mongoose matrimony microservice

## Primary Navigation Routes
- `/`: Home landing page with sacred hero slider, matchmaking steps, and member discovery
- `/about`: Company background, mission, leadership, and community values
- `/service`: Matchmaking services, verified astrology matchmaking, and personal assistance
- `/membership`: Transparent membership packages (Silver and Premium memberships)
- `/register`: User onboarding with sponsor lock protection and mandatory privacy consent
- `/contact`: Dedicated member support and grievance redressal contact points

## Legal & Compliance Disclosures
- `/privacy-policy`: Comprehensive privacy disclosures under DPDP Act 2023
- `/terms`: Terms and conditions governing matchmaking membership, age eligibility, and conduct
- `/cookie-policy`: Detailed cookie declaration and tracking consent choices
- `/refund-policy`: Policy governing digital service activation and subscription cancellations
- `/data-disclosure`: Detailed user personal data handling and disclosure policy
