# Sangam Sathi - Premium Matrimony Microservice Platform

> Sangam Sathi is a trusted, elegant matrimony application designed to connect prospective brides and grooms within communities with security, authenticity, and cultural dignity.

## Platform Features
- Verified Profiles: Rigorous photo and identity verification for authentic matchmaking.
- Community Centric: Filter candidates by caste, religion, education, occupation, and geographical locations.
- Membership Tiers: Transparent Free, Silver, and Premium memberships.
- Sponsor & Referral Ecosystem: Integrated promoter attribution and referral rewards with cryptographic registration locking.
- Privacy & DPDP Act 2023 Compliance: User-controlled profile visibility, grievance redressal, and full consent lifecycle management.
